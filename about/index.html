I am an MSc student in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Southern Illinois University Carbondale, advised by Prof. Ning Weng. I work on circuit design and on the security of the systems those circuits run. Most of my time goes to analog and mixed-signal VLSI, digital VLSI, and programmable ASICs. More recently I have been working on hardware security, machine learning, and what happens when LLM agents are given control of real systems.

I hold a Graduate Assistantship in the ECE department and work as a Teaching Assistant.
